# "THE GAP": script (approved: 45 s, Stomach scene cut, Chatterbox voices)

**Format:** about 60 s, 1080×1920, same style as Enamel v3: cut-away anatomy, googly-eyed organs, one-word captions, organ labels, hard cuts every 4–8 s.
**Core fact:** teeth don't grow back or grow longer. After an extraction, the neighbours **tip and drift** into the gap, the tooth in the opposite jaw **over-erupts** (slides out of its socket toward the gap), and the jaw bone under the gap **shrinks**.
**Interactive beats:** a "who moves first?" guess at 0:22 (drives comments), a fourth-wall question from Brain, and a comment prompt at the end.

## Script

- **BRAIN:** Red alert! Lower left molar is screaming!
- **MO:** I'm fine. I'm fine. I'm… so not fine.
*SFX:* alarm blip, pain sting

**SCENE 2 · 0:04–0:10 · The dentist chair**
*Visual:* wide shot of the mouth cut-away (Lefty, Mo, Righty, with Upper above), plus a corner inset of Heart pounding and Lungs puffed up.
- **HEART:** Dentist chair?! I'm beating SO fast!
- **BRAIN:** Lungs. BREATHE.
- **LUNGS:** *(exhale)* …sorry.
- **MO:** It's ONE tooth? I chewed for you for twenty years!
*SFX:* heartbeat, deflating balloon

**SCENE 3 · 0:10–0:16 · The extraction**
*Visual:* gloved hand with forceps; a wiggle-wiggle, then one clean POP. Mo is lifted out of frame, waving. Then an empty socket.
- **MO:** *(fading up)* Tell the bone… I loved it…
- **LEFTY & RIGHTY:** *(salute)* …
- **GUMS:** Patching the hole! Give us a few weeks.
*Labels:* "Gums", "Socket"
*SFX:* creak, POP, whoosh, tiny sad violin

**SCENE 4 · 0:16–0:22 · Tongue discovers the hole**
*Visual:* Tongue slides in and pokes the socket again and again (squash-and-stretch every poke).
- **TONGUE:** Ooh, a hole. Can I touch it? I'm touching it.
- **BRAIN:** Tongue! Stop poking the hole.
- **TONGUE:** Can't. Won't.
*Label:* "Tongue" · *SFX:* boing on each poke

**SCENE 5 · 0:22–0:28 · INTERACTIVE: guess**
*Visual:* freeze frame; the gap glows, and three arrows point at Lefty, Righty and Upper, labelled **A / B / C**. A countdown ring ticks for 3 s.
- **BRAIN:** *(to camera)* Quick. Who moves into the gap first? Guess in the comments!
*On-screen text:* WHO MOVES IN? · A · B · C
*SFX:* game-show tick-tock

**SCENE 6 · 0:28–0:40 · The land grab ("6 MONTHS LATER")**
*Visual:* a calendar flips. Righty tips toward the gap, Lefty drifts in "casually", and Upper slowly slides down out of its socket toward the gap.
- **RIGHTY:** Free real estate!
- **LEFTY:** I'm not moving. I'm just… leaning.
- **UPPER:** Nobody's biting me back… so I'm coming DOWN.
- **BRAIN:** It was a trick question. ALL of them.
*Labels:* "Drifting", "Tipping", "Over-erupting"
*SFX:* calendar flips, stretching creaks, ding on the reveal

**SCENE 7 · 0:40–0:46 · The bone shrinks**
*Visual:* close-up of the bone under the gap shrinking inward like a deflating cushion, with a sweat drop.
- **JAW BONE:** No tooth, no workout. I'm shrinking.
- **JAW BONE:** Up to half my width. In one year.
*Label:* "Jaw Bone" · *SFX:* slow deflate

**SCENE 8 · 0:46–0:52 · Stomach complains**
*Visual:* hard cut to the stomach cut-away; huge unchewed chunks drop in, *thunk thunk*.
- **STOMACH:** Who's sending me WHOLE chunks?!
- **BRAIN:** We chew on one side now.
- **TONGUE:** And food gets stuck in the leaning bits!
*Label:* "Stomach" · *SFX:* thunks, angry grumble

**SCENE 9 · 0:52–1:00 · Fix it + comment prompt**
*Visual:* back to Brain HQ; the whole cast squeezes into one frame.
- **BRAIN:** Lost a tooth? Ask your dentist early about filling the gap.
- **UPPER:** Or I keep coming down.
- **TONGUE:** And I keep poking.
*End card (two lines):* TEETH DON'T GROW. / THEY MOVE.
*Comment prompt:* GOT A GAP? COMMENT 🦷
*SFX:* upbeat sting

## Fact notes (why the wording is careful)
- Neighbouring teeth tipping and drifting into an extraction space, and the opposing tooth over-erupting, are well-documented consequences of not replacing a missing tooth.
- "Up to half my width in one year": studies of healing sockets report roughly half the bone width lost within 12 months, most of it in the first 3 months. Said as "up to", so it isn't overstated.
- "Ask your dentist early about filling the gap": deliberately no product claim. The options (implant, bridge, partial denture, space maintainer for kids) depend on the person.
- Speed is exaggerated for comedy: drifting takes months to years, so Scene 6 is labelled "6 MONTHS LATER".
