## Fact notes (why the wording is careful)

- **"Harder than bone"**: true. Enamel is the hardest substance in the body (~96% mineral).
- **Avoid "tougher than bone"**: hardness ≠ toughness. Enamel is hard but *brittle*, which is exactly why it chips.
- **"Cells that built it are gone"**: true. Ameloblasts are lost once the tooth erupts, so enamel can't regenerate.
- **Avoid "the only thing in your body that can't heal"**: false. Cartilage, inner-ear hair cells and most neurons barely regenerate either.
- **Nuance if commenters push back**: early mineral loss can be *remineralised* (fluoride, saliva), but lost structure (a chip, a cavity) can't grow back. The dentin underneath can lay down some repair dentin; enamel can't.
